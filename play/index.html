PERFORMANCE GUIDE

System Requirements & Reducing Game Lag

Anime Zero features dynamic lighting and particle spell effects. If you experience framerate drops or stuttering during massive boss fights, apply these optimizations:

PC System Recommendations

OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit or macOS 11+

Processor: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 or higher

Memory: 8 GB RAM recommended (4 GB minimum)

Graphics: Dedicated GPU with DirectX 11 support

Mobile System Recommendations

iOS: iPhone 11 or newer (iOS 14.0+)

Android: Snapdragon 720G / Dimensity 800 or better (Android 9.0+, 4GB+ RAM)

Storage: At least 1.5 GB free space for Roblox shader cache

3 Instant Lag Fixes

1. Press ESC in-game > Settings > switch Graphics Mode to Manual and set slider to 3–5.

2. Toggle off Screen Shake in the game options.

3. Close background YouTube or Discord streams to free up system RAM.

🛡️ Official Roblox Security & Anti-Phishing Warning

Always ensure your browser URL begins with https://www.roblox.com/ when launching the game. Never click unofficial Discord links claiming to grant "Free Robux Generators", "Admin Mod Menus", or "Private Developer Access". Roblox staff and Anime Zero developers will never ask for your password or your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie. Protect your account by enabling 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) in your Roblox account settings.

Is Anime Zero free to play on Roblox? +

Yes! Anime Zero is completely free to play. You do not need to buy access passes or pay Robux to enjoy the complete story dungeons, summon characters, or participate in raids.

Why is Anime Zero not loading or giving an error code? +

If the game fails to load (e.g. Error Code 279 or stuck loading terrain), try restarting your Roblox client or selecting a different server region. It can also happen during game updates when developers reboot live servers.

Yes. All Anime Zero hero rosters, inventory items, and level progression are stored on cloud servers linked to your Roblox username. Logging into the same Roblox account on PC, phone, or console will preserve all your saved data.
