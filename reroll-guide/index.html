ADVANCED MECHANICS

Trait Rerolling vs Character Rerolling

It is important to understand the difference between Character Rerolling and Trait Rerolling:

Reroll Type What It Modifies Currency Used When to Do It
Character Reroll Your base unit lineup (pulling new heroes from gacha). Gems / Summon Crystals Only at account creation on Day 1.
Trait Reroll Passive sub-stats (e.g. +30% Crit, Almighty, Monarch). Trait Tokens (from code ZERO10K) After you have already obtained an SS-tier character.

Never waste your Trait Reroll tokens on Rare or Epic units. Save all trait tokens until you successfully summon a Mythic character from our Tier List.
